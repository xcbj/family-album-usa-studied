No problem, Grandpa.（不）

Mr. Stewart. Stewart（斯图尔特先生。）

You think I can just skip it now and get to it at college?（你觉得我现在能跳过它，等大学再学吗？）

That must be Nat.（那一定是纳特。）

Where?（在哪？）
In town. At the hardware store.（在镇上。在五金店。）
I remember now. Right. Hi.（我现在想起来了。对。嗨。）
Hello, Alexandra.（你好，亚历山德拉。）

Did you read the story in the paper, Malcolm?（你看了报纸上的报道了吗，马尔科姆？）

It's a serious matter for a lot of us.（对我们很多人来说是件严肃的事。）
A serious matter.（严肃的事。）

Thanks. Nice to meet you ... again.（谢谢。很高兴又认识你。）
What's the problem?（什么事？）
Come on! You'll hear about it.（来吧！你会听到的。）

There is a way, Malcolm.（有办法的，马尔科姆。）
We get our friends to roll their sleeves up and get to work.（我们让朋友们卷起袖子开始干活。）
It's certainly a good idea.（确实是个好主意。）
If I could take a look at the place,（如果我能看看那地方，）
I could probably tell what it requires to fix it up.（我大概能说出翻修需要什么。）
How much paint, how many hours of work ...（多少油漆，多少工时……）
That's what I came to ask you to do, Malcolm.（这正是我来请你做的，马尔科姆。）
If you would supervise the refurbishing,（如果你监督翻修，）
I'll find the people to help do it.（我来找人帮忙做。）
I'll help too, Mr. Baker.（我也帮忙，贝克先生。）
I can get some of my friends to go around the neighborhood（我能叫一些朋友在邻里间走访，）

I'll help.（我帮忙。）
Tomorrow. Yes. We'll meet tomorrow morning, right here.（明天。是的。我们明早在这里碰面。）
Can we help?（我们能帮忙吗？）
I'd really like to.（我真的很想。）
Sure.（当然.）
We might need you to come through with your friends, Robbie.（我们可能需要你和朋友帮忙，罗比。）

I'll do it. I'll talk to them.（我来做。我和他们谈。）

Come in, come in. Please, come in.（进来，进来。请进。）
